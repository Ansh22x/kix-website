Engineering FAQs

Frequently asked questions

Direct answers about our development standards and project delivery.

Who owns the code upon completion?

You own 100% of the intellectual property and code repository. We hand over the entire Git repository, Dockerfiles, and configuration files upon milestone completion.

Where are the bots and software hosted?

We configure deployments directly on your preferred VPS (DigitalOcean, AWS, Hetzner, Railway) or provide turnkey managed container environments configured for 24/7 uptime.

Can you handle high bot traffic without rate limits?

Yes. We design our bots with asynchronous webhook dispatchers, Redis message queues, and smart rate-limit backoffs to process thousands of updates per second cleanly.

How do payments and milestones work?

Projects typically operate on standard phased milestone splits: initial architecture kickoff, functional demo review on test servers, and final deployment handover.
